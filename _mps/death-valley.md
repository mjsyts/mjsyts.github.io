## Overview
Death Valley is a cubic nonlinear waveshaper. It runs at 4x oversampling with first-order antiderivative antialiasing (ADAA), so it stays clean at high drive and with high-frequency inputs. A DC filter on the output removes the offset that **bias** adds.
